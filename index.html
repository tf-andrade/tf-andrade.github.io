About

I'm a Brazilian engineering leader with 15+ years of experience at the intersection of data, AI, and platform engineering. I've spent my career at companies where technology moves at scale — B2W Digital, Americanas, and Stone — and along the way co-founded an open source AI project that became an Apache incubator initiative.

I believe great engineering starts with people. My work spans data science, infrastructure, FinOps, and internal developer platforms — always with a product mindset and a focus on long-term impact.

When I'm not leading teams, I'm training for my next race. I've completed many sprints, standards and a 70.3 Ironman triathlon, and I bring the same discipline and resilience to everything I do.
